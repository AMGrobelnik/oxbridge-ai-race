Sealed Backtest: AI quant research that can't fool itself

An AI agent searches for a SOL/USDC trading rule on real Kraken prices. Before it backtests a single idea, its test, including market windows and stress scenarios it never sees, is hashed and anchored on Solana. When the search ends the seal breaks, every result is re-scored on the hidden test, and the overfit champion is rejected.
