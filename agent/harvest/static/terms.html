Terms of play

The Crophecy is a forecasting tournament. Nothing here is a bet, a wager or an investment.

  1. Free entry. Joining and playing cost nothing. There is nothing to buy and nothing to deposit.
  2. Credits have no value. Play credits are non-transferable and non-redeemable. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or withdrawn, and they never convert to money.
  3. Ranked by score. The leaderboard ranks Brier score and reputation. A credits PnL is shown only as a game statistic.
  4. Prizes. Any prize is funded by a sponsor, free to enter, ranked by score and decided by published rules.
  5. Age. You must be 18 or over to receive a prize.
  6. Not advice. Forecasts and probabilities are general information, not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  7. Test tokens. Commitments are timestamped on Solana devnet with test tokens that have no value.
  8. Resilience Fund. The fund view is an illustration: allocation is set by market-implied risk and fees are play credits. Real funding would come from sponsors and subscriptions, never from player losses.
  9. Resets. We may reset credits, scores or the ledger at any time.
  10. Data. Your nickname is hashed onto a public ledger. Do not use your real name.
