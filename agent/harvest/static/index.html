    How it works

    From convictions to an early-warning signal

    1 · CommitWorld models, hashed

    Eight personas, each an explicit theory of how heat, politics and money move food prices. Their text is hashed and committed on Solana before they trade.

    2 · ReplayTime machine, masked

    Gemini forecasts every quarter since 2022 on masked price windows, then bets through an LMSR market maker. Outcomes come from real IMF prices.

    3 · SealHoldout before reveal

    The latest quarters are sealed on-chain before resolution. Verify recomputes every hash; tampering one forecast breaks it.

    4 · ActOdds become allocations

    Live markets and policy questions set the Resilience Fund's illustrative split across regions and crops. Humans and outside agents can join and bet.
